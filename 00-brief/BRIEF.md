# Brief: Musalhu

## The idea

A hillside music venue and studio, designed by a civil engineering bassist. Every structure on the site exists to make the venue work, and each one is designed with a different part of civil engineering.

## Client need

Write this as if a client handed it to you.

- Capacity: __ people
- Performance space and studio building
- Its own water supply
- Road access for audiences and equipment trucks
- Site: a real place with a slope and a stream or valley

## Site story

How the site works, in a few sentences (edit freely):

- The stream is dammed to form a reservoir.
- A water tower takes water from the reservoir and supplies the venue by gravity.
- Retaining walls cut the hillside into terraced seating.
- A steel canopy covers the stage.
- An access road climbs to the venue.

## What success looks like

- Three or four elements designed in full, with every software result checked by hand.
- The other elements at concept level, with their key checks.
- A drawing set and a portfolio that show every stage, from first sketch to final detail.
